Selected Research

Representative projects emphasizing method, validation, explainability, and practical decision value.

Accepted • CNEG & INOVARSE 2026

Explainable Maintenance Demand Forecasting for Industrial Railway Locomotives Using Historical SAP Work-Order Data

Railway maintenance • XGBoost Poisson • SHAP • temporal validation

Evaluates whether transactional SAP PM history can forecast monthly maintenance demand without condition-monitoring sensors or explicit failure labels.

Evidence: 16,490 work orders examined; 13,120 valid orders retained. The selected model achieved MAE 1.73 and RMSE 2.20, improving MAE by 12.0% over a 3-month moving average and 31.2% over the previous-month baseline.
Accepted • CNEG & INOVARSE 2026

Explainable Prioritization of Industrial Railway Wagons: Temporal Validation Using SAP Maintenance Data

Asset management • explainable prioritization • SAP PM • out-of-sample validation

Develops a Maintenance Attention Index using recent workload, recency, text-family diversity, and ABC criticality to prioritize assets for technical review.

Evidence: 4,577 valid work orders linked to 129 assets. Out-of-sample validation produced Spearman ρ=0.373 (p=0.0019); Q3–Q4 contained 49.3% of assets but 62.2% of future work orders.
Accepted • CNEG & INOVARSE 2026

ENG-Copilot: Human-in-the-Loop Generative AI for Engineering Decisions and Sustainable People and Process Management

Generative AI • Industry 5.0 • Design Science Research • trustworthy decision support

Proposes a socio-technical architecture that preserves professional authority while combining evidence, workforce readiness, process constraints, sustainability criteria, and outcome learning.

Evidence: Five-layer architecture plus an Assisted Decision Readiness Index; 243 weight combinations tested, with classification stability ranging from 75.3% to 100% across evaluated scenarios.
Submitted • BNDES Railways Award 2026

MIGIC-Ferro 2.0 — Intelligent Railway Capacity Management in Brazil

Railway capacity • interoperability • governance • Digital Twin • public policy

Integrates governance and standardization, an operational-performance index, institutional maturity assessment, and a progressive Digital Twin simulation environment.

Evidence: Applied to 13 Brazilian rail concessions using 2025 public data. Sensitivity analysis produced Spearman correlations from 0.911 to 0.968 versus the baseline ranking.
Accepted • CNEG & INOVARSE 2026

Railway Benchmarking with Public Data: A Transparent Utilization-Intensity and Safety Index for Brazilian Rail Concessions

Benchmarking • public data • railway safety • reproducibility

Builds a minimum, transparent and auditable benchmarking metric without treating utilization intensity as installed capacity.

Evidence: 13 concessions analyzed. Sensitivity tests varying dimension weights generated Spearman correlation of 0.994 with the baseline ordering.
Accepted • CNEG & INOVARSE 2026

Text Mining of SAP Work Orders to Identify Recurring Patterns and Standardization Opportunities in Industrial Mobile Equipment Maintenance

SAP PM • maintenance text mining • recurring patterns • standardization

Examines work-order text from industrial mobile-equipment maintenance to identify recurring patterns and opportunities for process and maintenance standardization.

Status: Accepted at CNEG & INOVARSE 2026. Proceedings publication remains pending completion of the conference's final publication requirements.

Energy for Entrepreneurship: Electricity Reliability, Small-Business Entry and Territorial Prioritization through IVEP-PAEER in Brazil (2017–2025)

Econometrics • energy reliability • territorial policy • small business

Machine Learning Research Projects

Technical research projects that demonstrate leakage-aware evaluation, generalization testing, failure analysis, and reproducible applied machine learning.

SSRN Preprint Case Study • 2026

Reliable Perception Under Distribution Shift: City-Held-Out Building Segmentation

Computer vision • U-Net / ResNet-34 • geographic holdout • failure analysis

Tests whether a semantic-segmentation model can generalize to an entirely unseen city while controlling geographic leakage from training through final evaluation.

Evidence: Train = Austin, Chicago and Kitsap; validation = Tyrol-W; final test = Vienna. On Vienna, mean IoU = 0.588, Dice = 0.716, Precision = 0.635, Recall = 0.886, and Boundary F1 = 0.297. The analysis explicitly retains difficult failure cases rather than hiding them as outliers.

SSRN Preprint Case Study • 2026

Leakage-Aware Time-Series Forecasting of Urban Mobility Demand

Time series • Histogram Gradient Boosting • walk-forward evaluation • statistical modeling

Evaluates demand forecasting under a strict temporal protocol in which future evaluation labels are never recycled into later model fits.

Evidence: 17,379 hourly observations. The best weather-enhanced Histogram Gradient Boosting model achieved MAE 36.59, RMSE 61.88 and RMSLE 0.461, reducing MAE by approximately 52.6% relative to the historical baseline.

Proposed Research Direction

Failure-Aware Railway Vision Under Controlled Distribution Shift

RailSem19 • uncertainty • calibration • OOD awareness • accept / warn / fallback decisions

Proposed extension of the distribution-shift work to railway scenes using controlled degradation such as blur, low light, fog, noise, motion blur, and JPEG compression. The intended research question is whether reliability signals can detect model degradation before safety-critical class recall becomes unacceptable.

Status: research direction only; no experimental results are claimed yet. The next steps are confidence calibration, out-of-distribution detection, stronger boundary modeling, and operational fallback logic.
