Deployment-Aligned Evaluation with Temporal and Geographic Holdouts: A Cross-Case Empirical Study of Forecasting and Semantic Segmentation

Herislei Coelho Pimentel

Publication type: SSRN Preprint
Publication date: September 29, 2026
SSRN Abstract ID: 7538179
DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.7538179
ORCID: 0009-0001-0692-4015

Overview

This cross-case empirical study examines deployment-aligned evaluation across two heterogeneous machine-learning settings: time-series forecasting under temporal holdouts and semantic segmentation under geographic holdouts.

The study emphasizes leakage control, reproducibility, failure analysis, transparent deployment assumptions, and bounded generalization claims. Rather than proposing a new learning algorithm, the work focuses on the relationship between evaluation design and the scientific claims that can reasonably be supported by model performance.

Research Cases

Case I — Time-series forecasting: Capital Bikeshare demand prediction using a conservative temporal evaluation protocol and machine-learning models including Histogram Gradient Boosting.

Case II — Semantic segmentation: Building-footprint segmentation using an author-defined cross-city split of the Inria Aerial Image Labeling Dataset, with Vienna retained as the final geographic holdout.

Reproducibility

The associated public repository contains the deterministic Jupyter notebook, Python workflow, data-loading logic, model configurations, random-state controls, evaluation definitions, and supporting documentation.
