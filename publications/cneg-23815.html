Text Mining of SAP Maintenance Work Orders to Identify Recurring Patterns and Standardization Opportunities

Research summary

The research examines how SAP PM work-order descriptions can reveal recurring and temporally persistent maintenance activities in industrial mobile equipment. After data cleaning, 2,522 orders associated with 95 assets were analyzed. N-gram mining produced 505 candidates, of which 70 met historical robustness requirements. In a future temporal holdout, 70.0% of robust patterns reappeared compared with 18.6% of other candidates (risk ratio 3.76; Fisher's exact p < 0.001). Human validation is essential, and textual recurrence is not interpreted as failure probability.
