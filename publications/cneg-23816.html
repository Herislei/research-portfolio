Research summary

The research proposes a transparent and reproducible railway benchmarking index based on publicly available Brazilian statistics and railway regulatory data. The Railway Operational Performance Index (IDOF) combines freight production intensity per railway kilometer with an inversely scored accident index. Applied to 13 concessions, the baseline ranking placed EFC first (100.0), followed by EFVM and RMN (83.3). A 40/60–60/40 weighting sensitivity analysis yielded Spearman rank correlation 0.994 relative to the baseline. This is a comparative screening tool, not an official regulatory ranking.
