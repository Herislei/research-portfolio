Scientific Scope & Evaluation Boundaries

Models and graphs in the virtual lab are explanatory demonstrations, not automatically reproductions of the original experiments or operationally validated predictions.

The forecasting case evaluated 5,919 hourly observations under a conservative time-based protocol; the segmentation case used 576 sampled Vienna patches from an author-defined held-out city split. Evaluation scope and leakage controls matter.

Next steps: annotated real image/mask upload, patch-level metrics, robust cross-city evaluation, reproducible notebooks and documented model provenance.
