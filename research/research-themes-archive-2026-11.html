Research Themes

Three interconnected research directions

These themes build on engineering practice and applied research experience, translating operational challenges into testable scientific questions, reproducible methods and decision-support concepts.

RESEARCH THEME 01

Trustworthy AI for Asset Reliability

Data-driven maintenance decisions

Develop interpretable, uncertainty-aware learning systems that help engineers anticipate asset deterioration, forecast maintenance needs and make defensible interventions.

Research question

How can predictive models remain reliable under changing operating conditions and limited failure data?

Explainable MLTemporal validationUncertainty quantificationData quality & drift
Intended research contribution

Transparent risk and maintenance-demand forecasts

Reliability, Risk & Optimization

Decision intelligence for critical assets

Connect asset degradation, work-order demand, capacity constraints, inspection strategies and operational risks to improve maintenance prioritization and resource allocation.

RESEARCH THEME 03

Human-in-the-Loop Digital Twins

Engineering judgment with simulation

Explore progressive digital-twin architectures that integrate operational histories, simulation, human expertise and governance into safety-conscious engineering decisions.

Research question

How should human oversight, evidence and model limits be integrated into digital-twin-based decisions?

Digital twinsHuman-centered AISimulationResponsible AI governance
Intended research contribution

Decision-support workflows with traceable human oversight

From operational challenges to defensible decisions

The proposed research approach emphasizes credible evaluation and transparent claims rather than predictive accuracy alone. The stages below describe intended methodology—not completed experiments.

01Frame the decision

Define asset criticality, operational needs, constraints and measurable engineering value.

02Build reliable evidence

Audit data quality, control leakage and construct temporal or geographic holdouts.

03Model & explain

Combine reliability models, interpretable ML, uncertainty analysis and simulation.

04Evaluate for deployment

Test robustness, traceability, human oversight and decisions under realistic constraints.
